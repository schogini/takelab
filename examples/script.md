# Welcome to TakeLab

Your script, demos and cameras. One recording workspace.

Today we are going to explain a small Python program, demonstrate it in a real terminal, and keep our notes close to the camera.

## Read while you demonstrate

Select this Markdown file for your teleprompter. Adjust the reading width and font size, then start a comfortable scroll speed.

Open `demo.py` on the right. Your script stays here while the reference changes.

## Show the result

Open a terminal and run:

`python3 demo.py`

Pause, explain the output, and return to your reference. Use the laser pointer or highlight tools to guide the viewer.

## Ready for a take

Set your preview aspect ratio, position your cameras and record. Choose separate camera files when you want a clean screen video and more editing control.

Make something worth explaining.
